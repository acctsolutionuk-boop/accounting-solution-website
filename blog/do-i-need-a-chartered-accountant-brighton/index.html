Do I Need a Chartered, ACCA or ICAEW Accountant in Brighton?

By James Fitzpatrick, AAT licensed · Accounting Solution · Last reviewed 29 September 2026 · 3 min read

No. Preparing accounts, corporation tax returns, Self Assessment and VAT returns does not require a chartered, ACCA or ICAEW accountant. Only a statutory audit needs a registered auditor, and a small company is exempt if it meets two of three tests: turnover up to £15 million, assets up to £7.5 million, 50 or fewer employees.

What the law actually requires

In the UK, accountant is not a protected title. Anyone can prepare your accounts, file your tax return or run your VAT, and no membership of ICAEW, ACCA or any other body is needed for that work. The titles that are protected, such as chartered accountant, only tell you which professional body someone belongs to.

The main piece of work that does need a qualified person is a statutory audit, which must be signed by a registered auditor. Most small businesses never need one.

Does your company need an audit?

Test (financial years starting on or after 6 April 2025)Small company limit
Annual turnover£15 million or less
Balance sheet total (assets)£7.5 million or less
Average employees50 or fewer
Worked example. A Brighton marketing agency has turnover of £1.8 million, assets of £400,000 and 12 employees. It meets all three tests, so it comfortably passes the two-of-three rule. It is exempt from audit and files unaudited accounts. Nothing about that needs a chartered accountant.

You might still need an audit if your company is in a regulated sector, if a lender, investor or parent company requires one, or if your articles say so. Ask the person asking for it, and check the contract.

What to check instead of the letters

  • Are they supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, by HMRC or a professional body?
  • Do they carry professional indemnity insurance?
  • Have they handled businesses like yours, and can they show it?
  • Is the fee fixed and agreed in writing before work starts?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I Need a Chartered, ACCA or ICAEW Accountant in Brighton?

No. Preparing accounts, corporation tax returns, Self Assessment and VAT returns does not require a chartered, ACCA or ICAEW accountant. Only a statutory audit needs a registered auditor, and a small company is exempt if it meets two of three tests: turnover up to £15 million, assets up to £7.5 million, 50 or fewer employees.

Can a non-chartered accountant prepare company accounts?

Yes. There is no legal requirement for the person preparing statutory accounts or a corporation tax return to be chartered, ACCA or ICAEW qualified. Only a statutory audit must be signed by a registered auditor.

Which companies need an audit?

Companies that fail the small company tests, plus those in some regulated sectors or whose lender, investor or articles require one. A small company that meets two of turnover £15 million, assets £7.5 million and 50 employees is exempt.
